Frequently Asked Questions

How it works How does WargaReport get city issues fixed?
  1. You spot an issue (pothole, water leak, dark street) and snap a photo.
  2. WargaReport verifies the evidence and dispatches it directly to the responsible municipal agency (PAM JAYA, Bina Marga, Dishub, DLH, Satpol PP, Citata).
  3. A public countdown starts. Neighbors can upvote the report to increase priority.
  4. Field crews resolve the physical issue and post verification proof before closing.

Status Meanings

What does each status mean?

  • Submitted

    Your report was submitted and received by our intake router.

  • Received

    Verified by WargaReport and queued for agency dispatch.

  • In review

    Agency coordinators are assessing field safety and equipment.

  • Assigned

    A field crew or technician is dispatched to the street.

  • Awaiting your info

    Inspector needs clarification or an updated photo.

  • Resolved

    Physical fix completed and inspection statement published.

  • Closed

    Case completed and archived.
